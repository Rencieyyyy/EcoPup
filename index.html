Who We Are

About EcoPup PawFect Bites

It all started with one simple question: "What if we could give dogs a meal that is both healthy and delicious?"

Inspired by their love for dogs, a group of entrepreneurs created Pawfect Bites, a nutritious dog food made from fresh potatoes, carrots, and cabbage. Each ingredient provides nutrients that help dogs stay active, healthy, and happy.

From 3-month-old puppies to adult dogs, Pawfect Bites is made to provide nourishment for dogs of different ages and breeds.

Because every wagging tail deserves a healthy meal, Pawfect Bites is more than food — it's a little bite of love in every bite.

Our Process

How It's Made

1

Washing

Fresh potatoes, carrots, and cabbage are thoroughly rinsed to remove dirt and impurities.

2

Cutting

Vegetables are sliced into uniform pieces to cook evenly and blend well.

3

Mixing

Chopped vegetables are blended together into a nutrient-rich, well-balanced mix.

4

Shaping

The mixture is formed into bite-sized pieces that are just right for every pup.

5

Packaging

Sealed fresh in compostable packaging, ready to ship straight to your pup's bowl.
